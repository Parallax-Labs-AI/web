Introducing Parallax Squadron

Why stop at one agent when you can deploy an entire fleet?

Parallel Sub-Agents, powered by Parallax Squadron.

Parallax Squadron is a local orchestration program in Parallax App, designed to quickly, and easily deploy several sub-agents of our models. Squadron is named after the term for a group of fighter jets (because we like planes), and allows the simple deployment and management of multiple agents. Squadron is able to unlock the true capability of parallel inference, allowing Parallax models to delegate tasks efficiently.
