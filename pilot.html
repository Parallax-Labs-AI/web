Pilot vs. Competition
1 Million Token Context
Pilot, powered by Parallax Labs' Mini models, uses Gated DeltaNet architecture. This means that Pilot can run significantly more efficient than standard Transformer models. It can run its full 1 million context on approximately 5GB of VRAM.
Agentic Toolcalling
Pilot doesn't just provide an answer, it can take action, such as create or edit files, create folders, run commands, search the web, and more tasks with the Parallax App API.
100% Local, 100% Private
Pilot is designed for local on-device inference, meaning your data is safe with Pilot, and never leaves your device.
Powered by Parallax Mini models
Pilot is powered by our Parallax Mini tier of AI models, with scalability allowing for users to option higher tiers. Pilot uses Mini, with an extended 1 million context window for maximum understanding and capability.
